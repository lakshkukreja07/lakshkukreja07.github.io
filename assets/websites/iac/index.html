Ideas deserve

The IAC at Jai Hind College is a safe harbour for early-stage ventures — a space where raw ideas meet the structure, guidance, and momentum needed to become real businesses.

We are committed to building a wholesome startup ecosystem that mentors and supports budding entrepreneurs, and to contributing to the nation through innovation at the student level.
